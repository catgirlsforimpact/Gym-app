# Lift Log

A minimal workout tracker. Log exercises, read form tips, track your progress and keep notes.

## Features
- **Train**: start a workout, add exercises, log weight × reps per set, and reorder exercises on the day with the arrows on each card. It shows what you did last time, and a timer runs while you train. You can also repeat a past workout.
- **Progression**: each exercise shows your last session and a target for today. Work in one app-wide rep range (8–13 by default, changeable in Settings): add one rep each time, and once every set reaches the top of the range, the app suggests adding weight (+2.5 kg, or +1 kg for curls, pushdowns and lateral raises) and dropping back to the bottom. **Use** fills in the suggested weights. It's only a suggestion: whatever you type is what gets logged.
- **Rest timer**: starts on its own when you log a set's reps, or tap **Rest**. Adjust ±15s or skip. It beeps and vibrates when time is up and keeps running if you reload. Choose 1:00, 1:30, 2:00 or 3:00 in Settings, or turn off auto-start.
- **Earlier notes**: when you start a template (from Train, Plan or Repeat), the notes you wrote the last three times you did it show above today's notes.
- **Templates**: save a routine like "Push day" (exercises plus number of sets) and start it with one tap. Weights are pre-filled from your last session. Create one from scratch, from a workout in progress, or from any past workout, and reorder its exercises with the up and down arrows. An unfinished template is kept if the editor closes by accident; continue it from the Train tab.
- **Motivation**: cheering messages when you have one set left on an exercise, when you finish an exercise, and a celebration screen (with time, sets, weight lifted and any PRs) when you finish a workout. Each moment has its own pool of phrases in `phrases.js`, which you can edit. You can turn them off in Settings.
- **Plan**: a simple week planner, with no calendar to manage. For each day of this week or next, pick one of your templates, mark a rest day, or write a short note. Days you trained show as Done, today's plan appears on the Train tab with a Start button, and "Copy last week" repeats a good week.
- **Exercises**: 88 built-in exercises (32 that train glutes, and 13–17 for each upper-body group), each with 5–8 reviewed form tips ordered from setup to common mistakes. Every exercise lists all the muscle groups it trains, main one first (e.g. Romanian Deadlift: Hamstrings · Glutes), and you can filter by Glutes, Quads, Hamstrings, Calves, Adductors, Back, Chest, Shoulders, Biceps, Triceps or Core. Add your own exercises, edit the tips, and keep personal notes on each one (cues, machine settings, grip).
- **Progress**: monthly and yearly workout goals; workouts this week, month and year, week streak, average workouts per week and average workout length; a weekly chart you can switch between workouts, sets and weight lifted; sets per muscle over the last 7 days against the common 10–20 sets a week for growth; personal records (latest five and how many in the last 30 days); most improved exercises over 8 weeks; an estimated 1-rep-max trend per exercise; body weight; and full history.
- **Body weight**: log your weight in the Progress tab and see the trend and your 30-day change. One entry per day; logging again the same day replaces it.
- **Notes**: a journal for free-form notes. It also collects your workout notes and exercise notes in one place.
- Eight themes (Auto, Light, Dark, Pink, Green, Sunset, Plum (dark purple) and Crimson (dark with blood-red gradients)), kilograms, JSON backup export and import.
- PRs are detected when you finish a workout.

## Run it
No build step and no dependencies. Serve the folder with any static server:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
# open http://localhost:8000
```

To use it on your phone, host it (for example with GitHub Pages: Settings → Pages → deploy from this branch). Then open it in your phone browser and choose "Add to Home Screen". It works offline after the first visit.

## Data
Everything is stored in your browser's `localStorage` on the device. Use **Settings → Export backup** now and then, because clearing your browser data will erase it.

## Files
- `index.html`: app shell
- `style.css`: styles (light and dark themes)
- `app.js`: all app logic, views and charts (plain JS, SVG charts)
- `data.js`: built-in exercise library and form tips, generated by `tools/library.py` (run `python3 tools/library.py data.js` after editing)
- `legacy-tips.js`: earlier built-in tips, used once to keep your own tips when the built-in ones were rewritten
- `phrases.js`: motivational phrases
- `sw.js`, `manifest.json`: installable / offline support
- `icon.svg`, `icon-*.png`: app icon (white dumbbell on a pastel pink-to-blue gradient)
